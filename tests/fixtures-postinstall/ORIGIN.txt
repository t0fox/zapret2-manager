ORIGIN of the tests/fixtures-postinstall/ snapshots
====================================================

These fixtures are the post-install snapshot of the router AFTER restoring
the upstream zapret2 engine, taken on 2026-07-27 (commit 3917508 on the
infra branch, "tests/fixtures-postinstall — snapshot after upstream engine
install").

Source: remittor/zapret-openwrt releases — the upstream zapret2 engine
(2nd gen, v0.9.20260307-r1, aarch64_cortex-a53) installed on the device.

Engine-only snapshot: NO orchestra (excluded from the product), NO 1st-gen.
Collected by tools/collect-fixtures.sh (busybox pgrep -x replaced with pidof
for this target — pgrep -x does not match the process comm here).

The real engine config file is tests/fixtures-postinstall/opt-zapret2-config.out
(quoted values, single-line NFQWS2_OPT, profiles split by --new, <HOSTLIST>/
<HOSTLIST_NOAUTO> placeholders, --filter-l7). This is what the writer/stripper
self-tests are now built on.

NOT device-confirmed by this agent: the device is currently with another
owner. These fixtures are a snapshot of confirmed origin (collected from the
device post-install), but the RUNTIME behavior of the ucode writer/stripper
against the live engine is confirmed on the target via tools/smoke.sh, not
locally.
